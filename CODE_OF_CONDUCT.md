# Code of Conduct

Swoop follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Everyone here is donating their time.
- Assume good intent, and criticize ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Don't share anyone's private information, including window titles from someone else's screen.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

If something happens that isn't OK, report it privately to the maintainer,
[bastienff](https://github.com/bastienff), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may edit or remove content, or block people who don't follow this code, as described in the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
